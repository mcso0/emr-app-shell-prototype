DEN-362 - Human Review ready

Guardian Billing Ledger Sidebar Report

DEN-362 adds a guardian-centered billing ledger to the billing content aside. The staff can now review a guardian's payable, unpaid, and completed billing history across owned patients, filter the visible ledger rows, and select rows that feed the central checkout context.

Ledger 정보 구조

  • The billing module now exposes BillingContentAside through the module registry.
  • The content aside title is 보호자 수납이력 and is scoped to the selected invoice's guardian.
  • Each ledger row shows patient name, invoice number, activity time, billable order-line count, explicit status badge, billing guardian, and amount.
  • Ledger row checkbox selection uses the existing selectedInvoiceIds store state so the central checkout summary updates from the same source of truth.
  • The previous central 보호자 청구 묶음 and 합산/해제 temporary sections were replaced by a compact 선택된 수납 건 summary.

필터 기준

Default focus is 수납대기 + 미납.

  • Date quick ranges: 1주, 2주, 1달, 3달, 6개월, and 직접.
  • Status filters: 수납대기, 미납, and 완료.
  • Initial status selection includes 수납대기 and 미납; 완료 is available on demand.
  • The ledger uses the DEN-361 rule: only reason-backed unpaid/failed invoices are 미납.

공동 보호자 정책

  • 타 보호자 청구서 포함 appears only when the selected guardian shares at least one patient with another guardian in mock relationship data.
  • The toggle is UI-local in DEN-362 and does not change DB queries, RLS, RPCs, or remote data.
  • When enabled, the ledger can include invoices for the selected guardian's owned patients even if the invoice guardian differs.
