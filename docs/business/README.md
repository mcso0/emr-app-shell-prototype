# Business Docs

Business docs capture market, customer, sales, pricing, and metrics context.

Planned subareas:

- `strategy/`: positioning, product wedge, market bets, and long-term business direction
- `sales/`: demo strategy, sales scripts, customer-facing scenarios
- `customer-discovery/`: interviews, segment notes, buying criteria
- `pricing/`: packaging, pricing assumptions, and monetization experiments
- `metrics/`: business KPIs and operating metrics
- `decisions/`: durable business decisions

Current supporting files:

- `docs/business/changelog.md`

Historical sales demo documents live in `docs/archive/sales-demo/`.

Use `docs/_meta/intake-rules.md` when routing business instructions from chat.
